# Pianist asset

> Repository note: `build/` holds generated outputs (re-created by the build commands) and `sandbox/` was the project's local working area (check scripts, logs, review renders, critic reports). Neither is in the public repository; paths into them record where a result came from. See [docs/README.md](../../docs/README.md).

`pianist.glb` is the rigged pianist of the film, bound in his **seated playing pose** (the bind pose is the pose at the
keys, so linear-blend skinning errors stay small for the whole performance). `rig.json` is the rig contract computed
from this exact file. Everything is regenerated by one command:

```
pwsh -File tools/pianist/run_all.ps1 -Work build/pianist/work            # ~6-8 min on this machine
pwsh -File tools/pianist/run_all.ps1 -Work build/pianist/work -RebakeCloth   # also re-simulate the suit (then review it!)
```

The suit's cloth simulation is not reproducible run to run (Blender cloth), so it was simulated once, reviewed and frozen
in `tools/pianist/frozen/cloth_suit.npz`; every rebuild reuses it (p5 checks a fingerprint of the suit topology and the bind
pose and refuses a mismatched bake). Everything else is seeded or analytic.

## Frame and placement
glTF / three.js, metres, y up. Pianist frame: floor (lake bed) y = 0, white-key front plane z = 0 (keys toward +z),
keyboard centre (E4|F4) x = 0, the pianist faces +Z and his right hand (treble) is at -X, white-key tops y = 0.715,
bench top y = 0.47. In the film's rig frame the GLB root goes at `(0, -0.04, KEY_FRONT_Z)` (0.022) with no rotation
(`scene/src/pianist/pianist.js` does this and asks the piano module for `setBenchHeight(0.47)`).

## Contents
See `pianist.inspect.json` (per mesh: vertices, triangles, materials, influences) and `pianist.glb.pack.json` (packed
sizes). One skeleton of 163 joints (MakeHuman `default` rig, bones renamed `.` -> `_`, e.g. `finger2-1_R`), 4 influences per
vertex. 661k vertices / 1.016M triangles in the GLB (fix round 1; was 474.5k / 559k): hands 52.9k for BOTH hands = 26.4k per hand (was 13.7k for both; 3.9x), suit
outer 129k (was 33k), hair + beard 258k / 231k; 22.65 MB packed (textures 14.6 MB; the hand and suit normal maps are lossless).
The wrist joint sits at the wrist crease (moved 15 mm distally from MakeHuman's, with the weight blend shifted along).
Bind pose: middle fingertips over G3 (left) and D5 (right), 38 mm behind the key front, all ten tips above white keys
(`node tools/pianist/check_frames.mjs` prints the fingertip-to-key table).

Hand texture set (`hand_albedo`, `hand_normal`, `hand_orm`, 6144 x 3072, right hand left half, left hand right half):
**9.05 texels/mm median** (7.2 p5, 11.8 p95) over the hands; the normal map is stored lossless (lossy WebP lost a quarter of its fine relief), generated by `tools/pianist/py/hand_textures.py` from the mesh itself:
knuckle wrinkles (PIP/DIP/IP strongest), palmar and palm creases, extensor tendons and a seeded dorsal venous network as
soft raised cords (normal map only, never in the AO), the nail paint the plates sample (muted bed with streaks, subtle
lunula, paler onychodermal band, off-white free edge), cuticle and lateral-fold grooves, skin micro-relief and pores,
faint fine hairs, redder knuckles and fingertips; the CC0 albedo's 5-20 mm blotches are de-chromed. The nail frames are
geometric (fingertip cross-section harmonics, mirror-symmetric L/R, written to `nail_frames.json`) and shared with the
plates. ORM = (crease-cavity AO, roughness, nail mask); metallic factor 0.

Suit normal map (`suit_normal`, 4096^2, 2.1 texels/mm, lossless, `tools/pianist/py/suit_textures.py`): the proxy's UV islands are
its pattern pieces, so every island border gets a seam groove, a pressed seam-allowance ridge and a faint topstitch line
(sleeve head, side, back, inseam, lapel and pocket edges), low-amplitude cloth unevenness, and compression folds: the proxy's
UVs were laid out on the standing mesh, so the UV->3-D Jacobian of the seated, simulated suit measures in-plane strain;
where it is compressed by more than 10 % (inner elbows, lap, seat, the jacket skirt on the bench) broad oriented folds
(50 x 10 mm, up to 4 mm) run across the compression. The button discs (18-22 texels/mm) get a horn-button relief:
dished face, raised rim, rounded edge, 4 holes and a cross-stitched thread. The module adds a procedural 2/2 worsted twill
(0.85 mm pitch) and sub-texel skin relief on the hands below the maps' resolution, faded out where it would alias. The
pinstripe albedo is stored lossless (lossy WebP smears 1-texel lines).

Compression: EXT_meshopt_compression (16-bit positions, 12-bit UVs, octahedral normals) + EXT_texture_webp. The page
loads it with `scene/src/pianist/vendor/meshopt_decoder.module.js` (copy of three r186's).
