## 1. How the film is directed

**One evening.** The sun goes from just below the horizon (-0.8 deg: the afterglow right after sunset,
DESIGN.md section 2 as amended by the integrator, I-23) through the references' -1 deg look to blue hour (-4.5 deg, ref 1)
while the last chord rings: a monotone cubic through musically anchored keys (f0 -0.8, verse1 -0.9, chorus1 -1, chorus2 -1.05, bridge -1.1, climax -1.2, tag -1.4, finalA -1.65, coda -2.3, pedalUp -3.9, filmEnd -4.5), so time never runs backwards
across a cut. The rig (piano, bench, pianist, ripple emitters) is yawed per shot so the glow sits where the composition
needs it (core: `rigGroup.rotation.y = 120 + cheat`, pianist faces A = 60 - cheat). The world never turns, so a change of
cheat between two shots that both show the horizon moves the mountains relative to the piano. Measured (horizon in frame
at the end of one shot and the start of the next, cheat change over 20 deg): S05 -> S06 (0 -> -25 deg), S09 -> S10 (70 -> -6 deg), S16 -> S16b (-5 -> 120 deg), S18 -> S19 (110 -> 53 deg), S19 -> S20 (53 -> 0 deg), S20c -> S21 (142 -> 12 deg) and S23 -> S24 (18 -> -25 deg) - each of these cuts also moves the camera to another side or height, so it reads as a new direction, not as a piano turning in place.

**Cutting.** Every cut is anchored to the music (`section@bar:beat`, resolved through score.json at load); cuts are
quantised to the frame at or before the event. Most land on a bar line; these are placed inside the bar on a musical event: S05 (bar 12 beat 2.52, `verse1@8:2.5`) and S14 (bar 34 beat 3.52, `chorus2@2:3.5`). **Cut on the push** (`cutOnAnticipation`): when the
bar is anticipated - any voice (melody, bass, chord; not ghost notes) attacked up to 3/4 of a beat early and still
sounding across the bar line - the cut moves to the first attack of that push, unless that attack would split a melody
note already sounding for more than 150 ms (then the next attack of the push, typically the melody's own, or the bar
line itself): S09 (bar 23, 148 ms early), S10 (bar 25, 372 ms early), S16b (bar 40, 380 ms early), S20c (bar 56, 370 ms early), S22 (bar 59, 365 ms early) and S25 (bar 66, 552 ms early).
Cuts across a held melody note, on purpose (each shot's Why says why): S14 (bar 34, D5(106.46-107.59)) and S17 (bar 41, Eb5(124.89-128.05)). Cuts inside a score phrase (not on its first bar): S02 (bar 3), S05 (bar 12), S09 (bar 23), S14 (bar 34), S16 (bar 39), S16b (bar 40), S20c (bar 56), S22 (bar 59) and S25 (bar 66).

**Pacing follows the form** (measured average shot length per section, table 4): long single moves in the intro,
verses, pre-choruses and bridge; chorus 1 (8.07 s) holds its first hook statement in one 4-bar shot; chorus 2
(4.92 s) is cut denser, with a cut on the countermelody's entry and 1-bar shots on bars 39 and 40; the climax
(4.8 s) at least as dense, with a syncopated cut on the push into bar 56 and a move that arrives on the apex;
then the camera breathes out (tag, final A, coda).

**Hands** (the user's bar: "show the hands playing clearly"). A hand counts as readable in a frame only when all five
fingertips are in frame, the wrist-to-fingertip span is at least 110 px at 1920, every fingertip and knuckle is
within 3 px of blur at 1920 (thin lens), and at most 2 of the 10 points are hidden behind his body
(capsules) or the piano (arms, cheeks, fallboard, desk, rim, lid) - all from the solved performance, per frame.
Measured: the declared hand(s) are readable for **107.9 s = 46.7 %** of the film; any hand
108.2 s = 46.9 %. Longest stretches without a readable hand: 211.2-231.0 s (19.8 s, 13.6 s of it playing), 156.4-175.2 s (18.9 s, 17.5 s of it playing), 80.9-98.2 s (17.3 s, 17.0 s of it playing) and 130.4-147.0 s (16.6 s, 16.6 s of it playing).
The check does not test how bright the hand is against what is behind it; that is measured on the rendered animatic
(sandbox/camera/handcontrast.py, section 8). It does measure how much the two hands' screen boxes overlap (Measured: hands).

**Focus.** Hand focus sits at the harmonic middle of the depth range of the hand's fingertips and knuckles along the
lens axis (from the solved performance), so the near and far fingers blur equally; close-ups use f/8-16 so the whole
hand fits in the depth of field (the check above measures it). The box follows the hand like a focus puller (0.25 s
smoothing); for the left hand's stride and octave jumps (S08, S16b, S20c) a quicker puller is used (0.08 s smoothing,
leading by 40 ms; `focus.on.fast`).

**Faces** (DESIGN.md section 8). Measured on the solved head pose: no frame shows his face in frame and lit from the camera's side while the camera is 100 deg or more round from his back.
Cameras 100 deg or more round from his back: S06 (max 120 deg, 737 frames, face out of frame), S24 (max 124 deg, 639 frames, face out of frame). His head centre is in frame (or within 5 % outside it) on that many of those frames: S06 0, S24 0. DESIGN.md section 8 (amended 2026-10-22, camera C-01) allows |alpha| >= 100 deg only with the head out of frame by that margin, or with the lens at least 110 deg off the face's forward direction where the head is in frame: every such frame passes. Margin test (head pitched 10 deg higher than solved, for a re-solved performance): no shot would fail. Lacquer mirrors (music desk, open fallboard): no shot shows a lit face mirrored in them.

## 7. Using and changing the edit

- **Camera source.** The core imports `scene/src/camera/shots.js` and calls `shotAt(t)` once per output frame and
  `cameraAt(t, shot)` at the frame centre and every sub-frame time. Pins: `shots.js?score=<url>&handtrack=<url>` (or
  AG_SCORE / AG_HANDTRACK in Node).
- **Hands.** `scene/src/camera/hands.js` follows the solved performance through `scene/src/camera/handtrack.json`
  (`node sandbox/camera/handtrack.mjs --perf build/anim` as the last step of every perf ship). Loading FAILS when the
  default or pinned track is missing or its bar timing does not match the score (no silent switch to the score proxy;
  opt in with `shots.js?handtrackFallback=proxy`), and frame_check.mjs / animatic.mjs exit 3 when the track's perf sha
  differs from the performance they use (`--allow-stale-handtrack` overrides). Current: /scene/src/camera/handtrack.json (perf, perf 911e89912a88).
- **Camera module.** `scene/src/camera/index.js` pins the world's sun to the edit's arc and keeps diagnostics in
  `window.__AG_CAMERA`; load it as `AG.init({ modules: { extra: ['./camera/index.js'] } })` until it has a slot.
- **Anchors.** `'chorus1@2:3.5'`, `'17:1'`, `'f12.5'`, `'m10'`, `'end' | 'lastNote' | 'lastMel' | 'pedalUp' | 'filmEnd'`,
  offsets `'+0.5s' '-1b' '+2f'`.
- **Workflow.** Edit shots.js -> `node sandbox/camera/frame_check.mjs --json sandbox/camera/frame_check.json` ->
  `node sandbox/camera/shotlist.mjs` -> `node sandbox/camera/budget.mjs` (DOF sampling) -> lab stills
  (`sandbox/camera/lab.mjs` + `labjob.mjs`) -> `node sandbox/camera/animatic.mjs`.
